# __DISPLAY_NAME__ LLM Scoring Criteria

Score each paper from 0 to 1 on five dimensions:

1. Novelty: Does it introduce a genuinely new idea, method, dataset, or system?
2. Problem Significance: Is the problem important for __DISPLAY_NAME__ research?
3. Potential Impact: Could the work change research or production practice?
4. Paradigm Shift: Does it suggest a new way of framing or solving the problem?
5. Lasting Value: Is the contribution likely to remain useful beyond short-term trends?

Use the full 0-1 range. Prefer calibrated scores:

- 0.9-1.0: field-shaping or clearly exceptional
- 0.7-0.9: strong paper with meaningful impact potential
- 0.5-0.7: solid but incremental
- 0.3-0.5: weak relevance or limited contribution
- 0.0-0.3: not relevant or not technically substantive

Return concise comments. Mention why a paper is valuable, not just what it does.
